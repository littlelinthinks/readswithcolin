Zizhi Tongjian: A 1,400-Year Case Book资治通鉴：千年决策案例库

By Colin · Column · New case every two weeksColin · 专栏 · 每两周一个案例

I don't read Zizhi Tongjian as history. I read it as what it really is: 294 volumes of decision post-mortems — how resources were gathered, how alliances were built and broken, how power compounded and collapsed. Money and management follow the same logic today; only the costumes changed.我不把《资治通鉴》当历史书读。我把它当成它本来的样子：294 卷「决策复盘」——资源怎么聚集，联盟怎么结成又怎么破裂，权力怎么复利增长又怎么一夜清零。财富与管理的底层逻辑千年未变，变的只是服装。

壹Why为什么读

Why read it as a case book为什么当「案例库」读

1Every crisis is a decision post-mortem每个危机都是一层决策复盘

Before every collapse there were choices — who misread the situation, who priced the risk wrong, who doubled down too late. It is the richest dataset of high-stakes decisions ever compiled.每次崩盘之前都有选择——谁误判了形势，谁给风险定了错价，谁加注加得太晚。这是人类有史以来最丰富的「高风险决策」数据集。

2Human nature never changes人性从来不变

Greed, fear, reputation, trust — the same forces that moved courts and armies move markets and boardrooms today. Reading them at civilization scale teaches you to spot them early.贪婪、恐惧、声望、信任——当年推动朝堂与战场的力量，今天同样推动市场与会议室。在文明尺度上读过它们，你就能更早识别它们。

3Time scale teaches cycles时间尺度教你看见周期

A 1,400-year view makes booms and busts look like weather, not miracles or disasters. That calm is exactly what wealth-building and management both require.一千四百年的尺度，让兴衰看起来像天气，而不是奇迹或灾难。这种平静，恰恰是积累财富和管理组织都需要的东西。

贰Format怎么讲

How each case works每个案例的四段结构

原文The excerpt原文选段

A short passage, kept original — long enough to carry the tension, short enough to read in two minutes.短段原文，保持原味——长到装得下张力，短到两分钟读完。

拆解The unpacking白话拆解

Who wanted what, what constraints bound them, which information they had or lacked — the decision tree drawn out.谁想要什么，被什么约束，手里有什么信息、缺什么信息——把决策树画出来。

映射The modern mapping现代映射

Where the same logic shows up now: pricing, hiring, partnering, competing, compounding — wealth logic and management thinking, made visible.同样的逻辑今天在哪里上演：定价、用人、结盟、竞争、复利——财富逻辑与管理思维，看得见摸得着。

行动The action card一张行动卡

One question to ask yourself this week, taken from the case. Because a lesson you can't act on is just a story.从这个案例里取出一个问题，本周问自己。因为无法行动的教训，只是一个故事。

叁Syllabus篇目

First four cases on the bench首批四个案例预告

01The Partition of Jin — names and order三家分晋 · 秩序的名与实

Sima Guang opens the whole book with this case: when the name collapses, the order collapses. On brands, rules, and the cost of broken conventions.司马光用这个案例开全书：名分一倒，秩序就倒。谈品牌、规则与「破坏惯例」的真实代价。

02Shang Yang's reforms — the price of system design商鞅变法 · 制度设计的代价

The most successful and the most lethal reform in the book. On incentives, execution, and what happens when the designer can't exit.全书最成功也最致命的改革。谈激励、执行，以及「设计者无法退出」时会发生什么。

03Changping — the logistics of attrition长平之战 · 消耗战的账本

A war decided less by courage than by grain, roads, and cash flow. The ancient twin of burn-rate thinking.一场与其说靠勇气、不如说靠粮草与道路定胜负的战争。「烧钱率」思维的古代孪生兄弟。

04Red Cliffs — how the weak build alliances赤壁之战 · 弱者的联盟博弈

When you can't win alone, the alliance is the product. On negotiating from weakness without becoming subordinate.当你独自赢不了，联盟本身就是产品。谈弱势一方如何结盟而不沦为附庸。

The promise这个专栏的承诺

Read the case→extract the decision logic→map it to your own situation→write one action card 读案例→提炼决策逻辑→对照自身处境→写下一张行动卡

No hero worship, no cynicism — just 1,400 years of expensive lessons, translated into moves you can make this quarter.不崇拜权谋，不贩卖厚黑——只把一千四百年里那些昂贵教训，翻译成你这个季度就能走的棋。

This column reads a classic for decision-making and management insight. It does not retell history for its own sake.本专栏以「决策与管理」视角读经典，不做历史复述，亦不谈权谋厚黑。

History doesn't repeat, but it rhymes. Learn to hear the rhyme.历史不会重演，但会押韵。学会听懂这个韵脚。
