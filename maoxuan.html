Reading the Classics for Method毛选精读：把思想方法读成工具

By Colin · Column · Ten essays plannedColin · 专栏 · 十讲规划

Strip away the era and what remains is a set of thinking tools: investigate before you conclude, seek truth from facts, grasp the principal contradiction. These essays read the classics strictly as methodology — how to understand a messy situation and act in it — and rebuild each tool for work, business, and personal growth today.把时代的外壳剥掉，剩下的是一套思维工具：先调查再下结论、实事求是、抓主要矛盾。这个专栏只取思想方法与工作方法——如何认识一团乱麻的局面、如何在其中行动——并把每个工具重造为今天做事、做事业、做决策的用法。

壹Why为什么读

Why these texts still work as tools为什么今天还要读它

1It is epistemology with teeth它是一套「带牙齿的认识论」

Most methodology books describe; these texts were written under pressure, to actually decide. The method had to work or people would pay with their lives — that sharpness is why it still cuts.多数方法论书籍在「描述」；这些文字是在高压之下写的，为了真的做出决策。方法必须有效，否则代价是生命——这种锋利，正是它至今好用的原因。

2The problems it attacks are permanent它对付的问题是永恒的

Too much opinion, too little evidence; many problems, few resources; long wars against short patience. Every founder and every professional fights these battles weekly.观点多证据少；问题多头资源少；长期作战遇上急于求成。每个创业者和职场人，每周都在打这几场仗。

Scope note:范围说明： this column discusses thinking and working methods only — investigation, analysis, strategy as craft. It is not about politics, and it takes the texts out of no context they don't survive.本专栏只谈思想方法与工作方法——调查研究、分析、作为手艺的战略；不涉政治，不为任何立场背书，只把这些方法当作工具来打磨和使用。
贰Format怎么讲

The anatomy of one essay每一讲的四步结构

精读Close reading原文精读一段

One passage, read slowly — what it argues, and what question it was answering.一段原文，慢慢读——它在主张什么，它在回答哪个问题。

提炼Extract the tool方法提炼

Distill one repeatable tool: when to use it, what steps, what it forbids you to skip.提炼一个可复用的工具：什么时候用、分几步、不许跳过什么。

演练Modern drill现代场景演练

Run the tool on a 2026 problem: a product decision, a career fork, a stalled project.拿一个当下的问题来跑这个工具：一个产品决策、一次职业选择、一个停滞的项目。

误用Misuse warning常见误用提醒

Where the tool usually breaks in careless hands — and the check that keeps it honest.这把工具在粗心的人手里通常怎么坏——以及让它保持诚实的那个检查动作。

叁Syllabus篇目

Five of the ten essays, previewed十讲中的前五讲预告

01On Practice — knowing is doing《实践论》 · 知道不如做到

Why real understanding comes only from running the loop: try → fail → revise → try again. The original argument against armchair planning.真懂只能来自跑完闭环：试 → 错 → 改 → 再试。这是反对「沙发式规划」最早最狠的论证。

02On Contradiction — find the main one《矛盾论》 · 抓主要矛盾

Ten problems tug at you; one of them is load-bearing. How to locate it, and why everything else often resolves once it moves.十个问题拉扯你，其中一个是承重墙。如何找到它，以及为什么它一动、其他问题常常跟着松。

03Oppose Book Worship — no investigation, no say《反对本本主义》 · 没有调查就没有发言权

The deadliest office sin: quoting reports instead of walking the ground. Fieldwork as a personal discipline, not a consulting pitch.职场里最致命的罪：引用报告，而不是走进现场。把「跑一线」变成个人纪律，而不是汇报姿态。

04On Protracted War — strategic patience《论持久战》 · 战略耐心与阶段判断

You will not win fast; you must also refuse to lose fast. How to split a long war into stages, and give each stage its own scoreboard.你快赢不了，但也绝不能快输。如何把一场持久战切成阶段，并给每个阶段单独记分。

05On Leadership — from the masses, to the masses《关于领导方法的若干问题》 · 从群众中来，到群众中去

Take scattered ideas from the field, distill them into a plan, bring the plan back for correction. A complete loop for leading any team.把一线散落的想法收上来，提炼成方案，再把方案拿回一线校准。一个完整的带团队闭环。

The promise这个专栏的承诺

Close-read→extract one tool→drill it on a real problem→learn where it breaks 精读→提炼一个工具→在真问题上演练→弄清它在哪里会失灵

Tools, not slogans. Every essay must survive contact with a real decision — or it gets rewritten.要工具，不要口号。每一讲都必须在真实决策面前活得下来——活不下来就重写。

Seek truth from facts — then do something with it.实事求是——然后，拿它去做点什么。
