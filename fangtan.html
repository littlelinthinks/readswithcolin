Great Minds, Cross-Verified大咖对谈，中西互证

By Colin · Column · 3 notes liveColin · 专栏 · 已上线 3 篇

I read interviews the way I read books — for the mental models behind the person. Every great founder or thinker is a walking bookshelf: the books they keep, the biases they've beaten, the cycles they've survived. This column takes one long-form interview, strips the small talk, and cross-verifies its hard-won lessons against 2,500 years of Eastern classics — Zizhi Tongjian, the philosophers, the strategists.我把访谈当书读——读的是一个人背后的心智模型。每一位顶级创始人与思想者，都是一座行走的书架：他们反复读的书、战胜过的偏见、穿越过的周期。这个专栏取一集长访谈，剥掉闲聊，把里面撞出来的硬核教训，与两千五百年的东方经典互证——《资治通鉴》、诸子百家、兵法谋略。

壹Why为什么读

Why interviews belong on a reading site为什么访谈也进「读书站」

书架People are walking bookshelves人，就是一座行走的书架

The most concentrated form of "what to read" is a thinker naming what shaped them. A two-hour interview compresses a lifetime of reading into a syllabus you can act on this week.「该读什么」最浓缩的形态，就是一位思想者说出塑造了他自己的东西。两小时的访谈，把一个人一辈子的阅读压成一份你这周就能照做的书单。

复盘Quotes are decision post-mortems金句本质是决策复盘

A great line is rarely wisdom — it's a scar. Behind every "be obsessed" is a period of being ignored. We read the scar, not the slogan.一句漂亮话很少是智慧，它是一道疤。每句「要 obsessed」背后，都有一段被忽视的岁月。我们读的是那道疤，不是口号。

破妄Cross-verification kills vanity互证破除自嗨

Strip a Western interview down to its model, then test it against the East's 2,500-year record of human nature. What survives both lenses is worth keeping.把西方访谈压成模型，再用东方两千五百年的人性档案去审。两套透镜都活下来的，才值得留。

贰Format怎么讲

How each interview note works每一篇访谈笔记的四段结构

摘录The excerpt — verified only原文摘录 · 仅可核实

One line, kept honest. A verbatim quote only if it can be sourced; otherwise a clearly-marked paraphrase. No fabricated dialogue.只取一句，守住诚信。能溯源才用逐字引文，否则明确标注意译。绝不编造对话。

西方之术The Western tool西方之术

The behaviour protocol — High-Agency, First-Principles, the actionable habit the guest actually practises.行为协议——高主体性、第一性原理，嘉宾真正在用的、可执行的习惯。

东方之道The Eastern anchor东方之道

Where the same logic lives in the classics: Zizhi Tongjian's cycles, the philosophers' warnings on human nature.同一套逻辑在经典里的位置：《资治通鉴》的周期、诸子对人性的一声叹息。

习而新之Colin's synthesis习而新之

One action to take this week — because a lesson you can't act on is just entertainment.本周可走的一步棋——因为无法行动的教训，只是消遣。

The promise这个专栏的承诺

Hear the interview→extract the model→cross-verify with the classics→make it yours 听访谈→提炼心智模型→与经典互证→化为己用

No hero worship, no hype — just the models behind the people, tested against 2,500 years of recorded human nature.不捧人，不喊口号——只把一个人背后的模型，放进两千年人性档案里验一验。

Quote integrity: verbatim quotes appear only when traceable to a published source; everything else is a clearly-marked paraphrase of the guest's documented thesis. This column reads people as books — it does not retell interviews for their own sake.引文诚信：逐字引文仅在可溯源至公开出版物时才出现；其余均为对嘉宾已发表论点的明确标注意译。本专栏「以人为书」来读，不为复述访谈本身。

A person is a bookshelf that can talk back. Listen, then verify.人是一座会回嘴的书架。先听，再验。
