Reader, note-taker, letter-writer 读书人、笔记爱好者、写信的人

What this is 这是什么

Reads with Colin is a weekly letter on the books that change how you see the world — and what you do next. Every Thursday, one issue: a favorite passage, two quotes, a book of the week with two lessons to chew on, plus what I'm currently reading and a reading tip you can try this week. Five minutes to read; a week to think about.

It is not a book review. It is not a list. It is a letter from a friend — a weekly note on what I read, what I underlined, and what I'd like you to underline too.

Reads with Colin 是一封每周四发出的来信，聊那些"能改变你看世界方式、也会改变你接下来所做的事"的书。每一期都有固定的栏目：本周选段、两句名言、一本好书与两条值得咀嚼的洞见，再附上我正在读的书和一条阅读小贴士。五分钟读完，够你回味一整周。

它不是书评，也不是书单——更像一位老朋友每周四的来信：聊聊这周读到的、想到的，顺便把书里最锋利的句子摘给你。

Why I write 为什么写

Reading is easy. Remembering is the work. The hardest part of a book is keeping what you underlined, applying it on a Tuesday afternoon, and letting the rest go.

This letter is my way of fighting "I read it and forgot." The best input is input with an output attached. Every week I take the sentences that hit me and rewrite them in my own words; wherever I can't, I haven't yet understood the book.

读书最难的不是读，而是记得住、用得上、放得下——记住好句子，用上好方法，放下读过的执念再往前走。

这封信，是我对抗"读完就忘"的方式：最好的输入，是带着输出输入。 每周把书里最触动我的部分重新讲一遍，讲不清楚的地方，就是我还没读明白的地方。

"Read not to contradict and confute; nor to believe and take for granted; nor to find talk and discourse; but to weigh and consider." "读书时不可存心诘难作者，不可尽信书上之言，亦不可只为寻章摘句，而应推敲细思。"

— Francis Bacon, Of Studies — 培根，《谈读书》
