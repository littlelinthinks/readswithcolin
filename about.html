01WHAT THIS IS这是什么

Reads with Colin is a weekly letter on the books that change how you see the world — and what you do next. Every Thursday, one short issue: a favorite passage, two quotes, a book of the week with two lessons, plus what I'm currently reading and a reading tip you can try this week. Reads with Colin 是一封每周四发出的来信，聊那些"能改变你看世界方式、也会改变你接下来所做的事"的书。每一期都有固定的栏目：本周选段、两句名言、一本好书与两条值得咀嚼的洞见，再附上我正在读的书和一条阅读小贴士。

Five minutes to read; a week to think about. No ads, no spam — just one thoughtful letter from a friend. 五分钟读完，够你回味一整周。没有广告，也不群发杂讯——就是一封朋友寄来的信。

I also keep a blog on thinking & learning at thecolin.vip, and write a WeChat channel called 小Lin思考 — that's me; Colin here, 小Lin there, same person. 我也在 thecolin.vip 写关于思考与学习的个人博客，公众号「小Lin思考」也是我——这边叫 Colin，那边叫小Lin，同一个人。

02WHY I WRITE为什么写

Reading is easy. Remembering is the work. The hardest part of a book is keeping what you underlined, applying it on a Tuesday afternoon, and letting the rest go. 读书最难的不是读，而是记得住、用得上、放得下——记住好句子，用上好方法，放下读过的执念再往前走。

This letter is my way of fighting "I read it and forgot." The best input is input with an output attached. Every week I take the sentences that hit me and rewrite them in my own words; wherever I can't, I haven't yet understood the book. 这封信，是我对抗"读完就忘"的方式：最好的输入，是带着输出输入。 每周把书里最触动我的部分重新讲一遍——讲不清楚的地方，就是我还没读明白的地方。

"Read not to contradict and confute; nor to believe and take for granted; nor to find talk and discourse; but to weigh and consider." "读书时不可存心诘难作者，不可尽信书上之言，亦不可只为寻章摘句，而应推敲细思。"

— Francis Bacon, Of Studies— 培根，《谈读书》
