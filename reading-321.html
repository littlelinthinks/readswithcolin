The 3-2-1 Reading Method知行 3-2-1 读书法

By Colin · Sep 2026 · Adapted from Dan Koe's frameworkColin · 2026 年 9 月 · 改编写自 Dan Koe 的框架

Most books fade within days. This is the method I use to turn reading into action — one focus, two outlets, three resources. I rebuilt Dan Koe's 3-2-1 framework for readers, and I use it for every book featured on this site.大多数书，读完几天就忘。这是我把「读书」变成「行动」的方法——一个聚焦、两种输出、三种养分。它叫「知行 3-2-1」：知而不行只是听闻，行而不辍方为真知。它改编写自 Dan Koe 的 3-2-1 框架，为读书人而重造，本站的每一篇推荐都由它跑出来。

1 pick a focus → 3 feed on three sources → 2 force two outputs 1 先定聚焦 → 3 三种养分输入 → 2 两种强迫输出
3Resources养分

Feed on three sources — nothing else只从三种源头汲取养分，其余一律断掉

Information overload is the enemy of focus. Junk dopamine feels like learning, but it only fills the day. So I cap my daily input at exactly three channels — long, medium, and short.信息过载是专注的头号敌人。「垃圾多巴胺」的感觉很像在学习，其实只是填满一天。所以我把日常输入严格限制为三种渠道——长的、中的、短的，各一。

1 BookOne book一本书

The backbone. One serious book per cycle gives you a systematic framework and decades of distilled thinking. Read it slowly, at the pace of comprehension.主干。每个周期只精读一本严肃的书，它会给你系统框架和几十年沉淀的思考。以理解的节奏读，而不是翻页的节奏。

1 Long-formOne long video or podcast一个长视频或播客

A book is a single voice; a long conversation shows you how someone argues — the chain of reasoning, live. It adds a second dimension to what you read.书是单一声部；一场长对话能让你看到别人如何论证——推理链条的现场版。它为你读的书补上第二个维度。

1 Short-formOne short-form account一个短内容源

One writer you truly trust. It is not for deep study — it exists to spark ideas and show you fresh ways of saying things. When it stops sparking, replace it.一位你真正信任的作者。它不负责深度学习——只负责点燃灵感和示范新的表达方式。当它不再点燃你时，就换掉。

    Do this week:本周行动：
  • Mute or unfollow 90% of your feeds. Keep three.取关或静音 90% 的信息源，只留三种。
  • Adding a new source? An old one must go.想加一个新源？必须先淘汰一个旧的。
2Outlets输出

Force two kinds of output强迫两种输出

Input without output is poison — it becomes the feeling of knowing instead of knowing. Output forces your brain to find the pattern and rebuild the idea in your own voice.没有输出的输入是毒药——它制造「我知道」的错觉，而不是知道。输出会逼着大脑去识别模式，用自己的话把想法重新拼装。

WriteOne card, per chapter写一张卡片

While reading, pause and restate what you just learned — in your own words, in a note, a blog, or a post. Not excerpts. Rebuilding is the whole point.读的时候停一下，用自己的话把刚学到的东西重述出来——笔记、博客、社交平台都行。不是摘抄，重新拼装才是目的。

SpeakOne telling, per book讲给一个人听

After finishing, explain the core idea to a friend — or to a camera. If you can say it with feeling and logic, it is finally yours.读完之后，把核心观点讲给一个朋友听——或者对着镜头讲。能带着情感和逻辑讲清楚，它才真正属于你。

    The rhythm:节奏：
  • One chapter done → one card written.读完一章 → 写一张卡片。
  • One book done → one telling, out loud.读完一本书 → 讲一次，出声地讲。
1Focus聚焦

One real problem at a time一次只聚焦一个真问题

Never read "in general." Every 2–4 weeks, narrow your curiosity to one problem that is actually troubling you. Almost every human struggle lives in one of four eternal directions: health, wealth, relationships, happiness. Pick a specific one — then let it choose your next book.永远不要「泛泛地读」。每 2–4 周，把好奇心收窄到一个正真实困扰你的问题上。人的困扰几乎都逃不出四个永恒方向：健康、财富、关系、幸福。挑一个具体的——然后让它替你选下一本书。

    Do this today:今天行动：
  • Write down the one problem bugging you most.写下最困扰你的那一个。
  • Your next book must serve it. Solve it, then move on.下一本书必须服务于它。解决了，才换下一个。

The loop闭环

Read→write a card→tell someone→solve your own problem→write the journey down→share it with people one step behind you 读→写卡→讲出→解决自己的问题→把通关过程写下来→分享给比你晚一步的人

That last step is why this page exists. Every recommendation on Reads with Colin is a card that survived the loop — this website is what the method looks like when you run it in public.最后一步，就是这个页面存在的原因。本站的每一篇推荐，都是一张跑完闭环的卡片——这个网站，就是把这套方法公开运行的样子。

3-2-1 Thursday →→
The weekly dispatch: 3 ideas, 2 quotes, 1 question — what the reading turned into.每周四的通讯：3 条思考、2 段引文、1 个问题——读书长出来的东西。

One problem, three sources, two outputs. That's the whole method — the rest is showing up.一个问题，三种养分，两种输出。方法就这么多——剩下的，是去行。知行合一，从下一本书开始。
