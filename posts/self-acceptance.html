Self-Acceptance 认同自己

A reading note by Colin on Self-Acceptance by Stefanie Stahl. Colin 的读书笔记 · 《认同自己》 · 斯蒂芬妮·斯塔尔

Hello —

I recognized the "internal judged listener" pattern. Not born — built up by accumulated judgments.

Where low self-esteem comes from, and the way out.

Quote I marked

Don’t judge myself. It is enough to not be judged by others.

— Stefanie Stahl, Self-Acceptance

你好 —

一本能读得懂的心理书。

"我不好、我不行、我不配"的根源与出路。

我划下来的句子

不背扣我不。下不被别人扣就够了。

— 斯蒂芬妮·斯塔尔，《认同自己》
