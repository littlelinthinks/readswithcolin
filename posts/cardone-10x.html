Cardone's 10X and the Art of Borrowing Momentum狂飙与定力：从 Grant Cardone 的 10X 看东方的「借势与修身」

East–West Synthesis · Grant Cardone · High Agency · Sep 2026中西互证 · Grant Cardone · 高主体性 · 2026 年 9 月

A high-agency operator who built billions by refusing the local optimum — read against the strategies of survival that Zizhi Tongjian records from China's most chaotic age.一个靠"拒绝局部最优"垒起数十亿的高主体性操盘手——放进《资治通鉴》记录的乱世求存策略里，对照着读。

Grant Cardone is easy to dismiss and easy to love — which is exactly why he's worth reading carefully. Behind the loud sales persona sits a simple, repeatable thesis: set targets ten times what you think you want, and take ten times the action you think is required. Refuse the comfortable local optimum. The East would call the same move 借势 — borrowing momentum instead of fighting it.Grant Cardone 既容易被人轻视，也容易被人追捧——而这恰恰是他值得被认真读的原因。在那副喧闹的销售人设背后，是一套简单、可复用的命题：把目标定到你认为想要的十倍，并采取你认为所需十倍的行动。拒绝那个舒适的"局部最优"。东方会把同一招叫做"借势"——借力打力，而不是硬扛。

The Axiom: 10X and "Be Obsessed or Be Average"壹 · 公理：10X 与「要么痴迷，要么平庸」

Cardone's two most documented ideas are the 10X Rule and the line that became a book title: be obsessed or be average. The West reaches for massive, disproportionate action to break through; the East reaches for 定力 — the steadiness to keep showing up when no one is clapping.Cardone 最被反复印证的两条主张，是"10X 法则"和那句成了书名的话：要么痴迷，要么平庸。西方用超量、不成比例的行动去撕开缺口；东方则用"定力"——在没有人鼓掌时，依然持续到场的那份稳。

访谈互证 · Cardone × 通鉴 Context Reflection

当 Cardone 的"痴迷"遇见通鉴里的"乱世定力"

思想源头：Grant Cardone《Be Obsessed or Be Average》核心论点 × 《资治通鉴》乱世破局者

"Be obsessed or be average."

Colin 的翻译与中西文明互证

"要么痴迷，要么平庸。"这是 Cardone 一本公开著作的书名，也是他反复宣讲的核心命题（引自《Be Obsessed or Be Average》，意译其主旨）。硅谷与销售圈用"痴迷式的超量行动"去撕开缺口；而《资治通鉴》里那些在乱世真正活下来、乃至翻盘的破局者，靠的同样不是聪明，而是一种"被所有人反对也不改其志"的定力。区别在于：西方的痴迷若只对外狂飙，容易变成自毁；东方的定力若只对内收敛，容易变成躺平。真正厉害的人，是把两者拧成一股——对外借势狂飙，对内修身守锚。读书、听访谈，是为了看清这股劲该往哪使。

— Grant Cardone，《Be Obsessed or Be Average》（书名即主旨，意译）

Three Core Extractions贰 · 核心提炼

The Western Tool · 西方之术
Refuse the local optimum. Cardone's protocol is to 10X the target and the action so that rejection, silence, and failure simply get absorbed by the volume — you out-act the noise.拒绝局部最优。Cardone 的协议是：把目标和行动都放大十倍，于是拒绝、沉默与失败，都被这个体量吸收掉——你用行动量淹没了噪音。
The Eastern Anchor · 东方之道
Zizhi Tongjian shows again and again: in a chaotic age, those who last are not the loudest but the steadiest — they borrow the tide (借势) instead of fighting it, and keep their 锚 (anchor) when surrounded by noise.《资治通鉴》无数次证明：乱世里活得久的，不是嗓门最大的，而是最稳的——他们借潮水之力（借势）而非逆潮硬扛，并在四面噪音中守住自己的"锚"。
Colin's Synthesis · 习而新之
Borrow momentum outward, guard your anchor inward. Fire without a steady hand just burns the operator. Discipline is what turns 10X from a slogan into a system.对外借势狂飙，对内修身守锚。没有定力托底的开火，烧的是自己。纪律，才是把"10X"从口号变成系统的东西。

This Week's Mental Iteration叁 · 本周心智迭代

学 · Ingestion
Take apart Cardone's "rejection as fuel" protocol — how a high-agency person metabolises "no" instead of fearing it.拆解 Cardone 的"把拒绝当燃料"协议——一个高主体性的人，如何把"不"消化掉，而不是害怕它。
忘 · Deconstruction
Forget the loudness. Strip the persona and keep only the discipline: out-act the noise, but stay anchored.忘掉那副喧闹。剥掉人设，只留纪律：用行动淹没噪音，但心要有锚。
习 · Action
Pick one area where you've settled for the local optimum, and 10X the action for one week — while writing down the anchor that keeps it from burning you.选一个你已"将就于局部最优"的领域，用十倍行动试一周——同时写下那个能防止它烧到你的"锚"。

Note on sourcing: "Be Obsessed or Be Average" is the published title of Cardone's 2016 book and states his core thesis; the 10X Rule refers to his 2011 book of the same name.
