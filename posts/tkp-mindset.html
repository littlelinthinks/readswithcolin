The Knowledge Project and the Latticework of Mental Models常青心智模型：从 TKP 看东方的「博学而约取」

East–West Synthesis · The Knowledge Project · Mental Models · Sep 2026中西互证 · The Knowledge Project · 心智模型 · 2026 年 9 月

The Knowledge Project sits down with the best decision-makers alive and asks, quietly, how they think. The pattern is always the same: they read widely and reason with a latticework of mental models — exactly what the East called 博学而约取.The Knowledge Project（TKP）请来当今最会做决定的人，轻声问一句：你是怎么想的。答案总是同一个：广读，并用一套"心智模型格栅"去推理——这正是东方说的"博学而约取"。

The Knowledge Project, hosted by Shane Parrish of Farnam Street, is one of the few interview shows that treats a conversation as a dissection of how someone thinks rather than what they've done. The recurring lesson from its guests is not a tactic — it's a stance: build a latticework of mental models from many disciplines, and let them check each other. One lens is a bias; many lenses are a compass.The Knowledge Project（TKP），由 Farnam Street 的 Shane Parrish 主持，是少数把访谈当成"解剖一个人怎么想"、而非"他干了什么"的节目。嘉宾们反复给出的教训，不是某个技巧，而是一种姿态：从多个学科搭起一套"心智模型格栅"，让它们彼此校验。一个透镜是偏见，多个透镜才是指南针。

The Axiom: A Latticework of Mental Models壹 · 公理：心智模型的格栅

The West reaches for many disciplines — physics, biology, economics, psychology — wired together so no single framework owns the answer. The East reaches for 博学之，审问之，慎思之，明辨之，笃行之 — to learn broadly, question closely, reflect deeply, distinguish clearly, and then act.西方人从物理、生物、经济、心理多个学科接线成网，让没有任何单一框架独占答案。东方则用「博学之，审问之，慎思之，明辨之，笃行之」——广学、细问、深省、明辨，然后行动。

访谈互证 · TKP × 诸子 Context Reflection

当 TKP 的"心智模型"遇见《中庸》的"博学审问"

思想源头：Charlie Munger「心智模型格栅」× 《礼记·中庸》博学审问 · 《资治通鉴》兼听则明

"You've got to have models in your head. And you've got to array your experience — both vicarious and direct — on this latticework of models."

Colin 的翻译与中西文明互证

"你脑子里得装着模型。并且要把你的经验——无论亲历还是间接——都挂到这张心智模型的格栅上。"这是查理·芒格（Charlie Munger）1994 年在南加州大学著名的演讲原话，也是 TKP 这类节目真正的思想地基。西方用"多模型互相校验"防止单一框架的盲区；东方则用《中庸》的"博学之，审问之，慎思之，明辨之"与《资治通鉴》里魏徵那句"兼听则明，偏信则暗"，早把同一件事说透了：只信一个角度，就是偏信；让多个角度互相打架，才看得见真相。读书、听访谈，不是为了收集金句，而是为了给自己的格栅，多挂一根结实的横梁。

— Charlie Munger, USC 演讲 (1994)（逐字可核实）

Three Core Extractions贰 · 核心提炼

The Western Tool · 西方之术
Build a latticework. TKP's guests don't rely on one framework — they wire physics, incentives, and psychology together so no single bias can own the decision.搭起格栅。TKP 的嘉宾不依赖单一框架——他们把物理、激励、心理接线成网，让任何单一偏见都无法独占决策。
The Eastern Anchor · 东方之道
Zizhi Tongjian records Wei Zheng's warning — 兼听则明，偏信则暗 (heed broadly and you see clearly; trust one side and you go blind). The classics prescribe the same multi-lens discipline 1,300 years before Munger.《资治通鉴》记下魏徵的警示——「兼听则明，偏信则暗」。在芒格之前一千三百年，经典就已经规定了同一套"多透镜"纪律。
Colin's Synthesis · 习而新之
Read one book from a field you'd never pick, then ask which of your old beliefs it pokes a hole in. That hole is the dividend.去读一本你绝不会主动选的领域之书，然后问它戳穿了你哪个旧信念。那个洞，就是红利。

This Week's Mental Iteration叁 · 本周心智迭代

学 · Ingestion
Take apart one TKP conversation for the mental models named — not the stories, the models.拆解一期 TKP 访谈里被点名的"心智模型"——不是故事，是模型。
忘 · Deconstruction
Forget the comfort of a single framework. The expert who trusts one lens is the easiest to fool.忘掉"只信一个框架"的安逸。只信一个透镜的专家，最容易被骗。
习 · Action
Add one cross-disciplinary book to this month's shelf, and write the single belief it challenges.本月书架上加一本跨学科的书，并写下它挑战的那一个信念。
