RWC #002 · Mental Models · 5 min read

The Great Mental Models 思考的框架

A reading note by Colin on The Great Mental Models by Shane Parrish. Colin 的读书笔记 · 《思考的框架》 · 沙恩·帕里什

Hello —

I use reverse-thinking and probability thinking every week. Before a meeting I ask: how would I make this fail?

First-principles, second-order thinking, the map is not the territory.

Quote I marked

When all you have is a hammer, everything looks like a nail. But check first.

— Shane Parrish, The Great Mental Models

你好 —

不是讲“什么是思维模型”的认知书，而是讲“哪些思维模型最值得收藏”的工具手册。

第一性原理、二阶思维、地图不等于疆域。

我划下来的句子

当你手里有把锤子，你看什么都像钉子。但首先看看是不是钉子。

— 沙恩·帕里什，《思考的框架》
