Learn, Then Transcend: A Reading OS Fusing Charlie Munger with Zizhi Tongjian习而新之：将查理·芒格与《资治通鉴》融为一体的阅读操作系统

East–West Synthesis · Charlie Munger · Zizhi Tongjian · Sep 2026中西互证 · 查理·芒格 · 资治通鉴 · 2026 年 9 月

The West builds lattices of mental models from the outside in; the East folds the mind from the inside out through its histories. What if we ran both operating systems at once?西方由外而内地搭建思维栅格，东方借史书由内而外地折叠心智。若两套“阅读操作系统”同时运行，会发生什么？

Western thinkers build lattices of mental models to navigate the world; Chinese historians folded two thousand years of rise and fall into a single mirror for the mind. Charlie Munger and Sima Guang never met — yet both were obsessed with the same question: how does a person actually make a good decision?西方思想家搭建思维模型栅格以驾驭世界；中国史家把两千年的兴亡起落折成一面照心的镜子。查理·芒格与司马光素未谋面，却纠缠于同一个问题：一个人究竟如何做出真正好的决策？

This piece is not a book summary. It is an attempt to run two reading operating systems at once — Munger's "by the outside in" lattice, and the Zizhi Tongjian's "by the inside out" mental folding.这篇不是书摘。它尝试同时运行两套“阅读操作系统”——芒格“由外而内”的栅格，与《资治通鉴》“由内而外”的心智折叠。

Part One · When Munger Meets Sima Guang壹 · 当芒格遇见司马光

Munger's lifelong creed was that wisdom is multidisciplinary — you must borrow from physics, biology, and history to escape the man-with-a-hammer syndrome. Sima Guang's creed was that history is the ultimate multidisciplinary textbook: every folly and every triumph is already on record.芒格毕生的信条是：智慧必须跨学科——你得从物理、生物、历史里借工具，才不至于“手里拿锤子，看什么都像钉子”。司马光的信条则是：历史本就是最博大的跨学科教科书，每一场愚蠢与辉煌都已被写下。

当芒格的栅格遇见通鉴的兴衰

思想源头：查理·芒格《穷查理宝典》 × 《资治通鉴》心智映射

"In my whole life, I have known no wise people who didn't read all the time — none, zero."

Colin 的翻译与跨文化感悟

“在我的一生中，我认识的所有聪明人，没有一个不是在时刻阅读的——一个都没有。”芒格终生追求的“多元思维模型”，其本质与我们翻阅《资治通鉴》、格物致知以洞察世事的路径完全一致：西方由外而内地搭建栅格，东方由内而外地折叠心智。学而知之，唯有内求。

Colin's Takeaway: Stop collecting books as trophies. Start running them through two engines — a Western lattice for structure, an Eastern mirror for temperament.柯林的话：别再把书当战利品收集。开始让两套引擎同时运转——西方的栅格负责结构，东方的镜子负责性情。

Part Two · The Three-Line Reading Loop贰 · 三行核心闭环

After every deep reading session, I close the book and run this three-line loop — a synthesis ritual that fuses Munger's output discipline with the historian's long view.每次深度阅读后，我合上书，跑一遍这三行闭环——一套将芒格的“输出纪律”与史家的“长时段视野”融为一体的合成仪式。

Protagonist · 谁是主角
Those who kept their composure across market cycles and historical tides.那些在周期与浪潮中始终保持定力、清醒自持的人。
Why it matters · 为何重要
We read history and books not to memorize dogma, but to own a ready-made map of first principles for the next crisis.读史与读书，不是为了记住陈旧的教条，而是为了在明天遭遇危机时，拥有一张现成的底层决策图谱。
Tomorrow's Action · 明日行动
Learn to know, forget to break. Tomorrow, strip emotion from your decisions and switch on second-order reason.学而知之，忘而破之。明天做决策时，主动剥离情绪干扰，启用理性的二阶思维。
