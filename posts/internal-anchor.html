Internal Anchor · East-Meets-West习而新之 · 全球心智书架

When Modern Psychology Met the Eastern Path of Liberation当现代心理学撞上东方解脱道——重构高 Agency 的内在锚

East–West Synthesis · The Diary of a CEO · High Agency · Sep 2026中西互证 · The Diary of a CEO · 高主体性 · 2026 年 9 月

Modern people buy "mindfulness protocols" as external tools; the East cultivated "stable wisdom through inner inquiry" as a way of being. Same panic, two thousand years apart — only the packaging changed.现代人把“正念协议”当外部工具追逐；东方先贤把“定慧内求”当作存在方式修行。同样的焦虑，隔两千年，只是包装换了。

While listening to The Diary of a CEO interview cognitive psychologists on escaping the burnout of short-video feeds and algorithmic noise, what surfaced in my mind was not a Western framework — but the ancient sages of Zizhi Tongjian, unmoved "even as Mount Tai collapsed before them."在收听《The Diary of a CEO》采访顶尖心理学家、谈论如何摆脱短视频与算法带来的精神内耗时，我脑海中浮现的并非某个西方框架，而是《资治通鉴》里那些在生死博弈、社稷倾覆前夕依然“泰山崩于前而色不变”的古之大贤。

Modern people buy "mindfulness protocols" as external tools; the East cultivated "stable wisdom through inner inquiry" as a way of being. Same panic, two thousand years apart — only the packaging changed.现代人把“正念协议”当外部工具追逐；东方先贤把“定慧内求”当作一种存在方式修行。同样的焦虑，隔了两千年，只是包装换了。

The Mirror: When DOAC Meets the Sages壹 · 当 DOAC 遇见古贤

The West reaches for protocols; the East reaches inward. Both are answering the same question the sages answered two millennia ago — how to keep the mind from being hijacked.西方向外求协议，东方向内求定力。二者回答的是同一个问题——如何让心智不被劫持，而这正是古贤两千年前的答案。

思想源头：爱比克泰德《手册》 × 《资治通鉴》心智映射

"Men are disturbed not by things, but by the views which they take of things."

Colin 的翻译与中西文明互证

“人不是被事物本身所扰，而是被他们对事物所持的看法所扰。”现代人追逐的正念与专注力 protocols（器），本质上正是东方先贤千百年来修身齐家的“定慧内求（道）”。爱比克泰德早在两千年前便点破：真正困住我们的，从来不是外界的风暴，而是我们解读风暴的方式——这与《资治通鉴》中“泰山崩于前而色不变”的定力，东西辉映。

— 爱比克泰德（Epictetus），《手册》（Enchiridion）第 5 节

Three Core Extractions贰 · 核心提炼

The Western Trap · 海外大咖的痛点
Modern tech and dopamine loops keep humans in chronic fight-or-flight; people search outward for fixes, yet grow more fragile.现代科技与多巴胺机制让人长期处于“应激状态”（Fight-or-Flight），人们不断向外寻找解法，却变得更加脆弱。
The Eastern Anchor · 东方经典的解法
Zizhi Tongjian proves again and again: the ultimate capability of great achievers is the "homeostasis" of their mental system, not how much information they hold.《资治通鉴》无数次证明，成大事者的终极核心能力不是掌握了多少信息，而是其心智系统的“稳态”。内观自己，格物致知，方能不被洪流卷走。
Colin's Synthesis · 习而新之
Reading and listening are never to stuff the brain with English quotes, but to add an "immunity shield" to your own life operating system.读书、听播客绝不是为了给大脑塞满英文金句，而是为了在自己的人生操作系统里增加一块“免疫盾牌”。
