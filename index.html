Atomic Habits, identity change and the quiet power of 1% 《原子习惯》：身份改变，与 1% 的安静力量

Why the smallest choices, repeated daily, end up changing who you are. 微小的选择日复一日，最终改变了你是谁。

James Clear's most-quoted idea — 1% better every day compounds into 37x in a year — is also his most quietly radical. 詹姆斯·克利尔最常被引用的那句话——每天进步 1%，一年变 37 倍——也是他最安静、却最深刻的一个洞见。

Thinking, Fast and Slow — lazy brains and the anchors in your head 《思考，快与慢》：偷懒的大脑，与你脑中的锚

Kahneman's two systems — and why the slower one deserves more of your week. 卡尼曼的两个系统——以及为什么你的一周里，应该把更多时间留给慢的那个。

System 1 is fast and confident; System 2 is slow and tired. Knowing which one is driving is half the battle. 系统 1 又快又自信；系统 2 又慢又累。知道此刻是哪一位在开车，就赢了一半。

Walden — deliberate living and the luxury of enough 《瓦尔登湖》：清醒地生活，与"足够"的奢侈

Thoreau's two-year experiment in simplifying life — and what still survives 170 years later. 梭罗在湖畔的两年实验——以及 170 年后，依然没被时间磨掉的几句话。

Walden is not about leaving society. It is about choosing what to leave out — and finding that's where the life is. 《瓦尔登湖》讲的并不是逃离社会，而是选择"留下什么"——而生活的全部意义，往往就在那些被留下的部分里。
